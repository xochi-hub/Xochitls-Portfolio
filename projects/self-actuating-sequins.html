Self-actuating Sequins

2026 · Morphing Textiles · Soft Actuation

E-Sequins are self-actuating sequins that move without someone touching them. Traditional sequins only flip between two sides when brushed by hand, so we explored how sequins could move on their own with continuous motion. Built in the Morphing Matters Lab under Professor Lining Yao, each sequin is 3D printed from TPU with a magnet inside. The magnets repel each other, so a small push on the first sequin sets off a chain reaction down the line, like dominoes. An electromagnet controlled by a microcontroller triggers the push. E-Sequins could be used to display data on the body, such as pace or distance, or for performance and fashion where the surface responds to movement.

  • Designed 3D-printed TPU sequins with embedded magnets in alternating polarities, so neighboring sequins repel and a small push on the first sequin flips the rest in a chain reaction
  • Explored applications for E-Sequins in wearable technology and performance fashion
  • Built a rigid test stand to compare magnet sizes, spacing, and placement, and developed optimizable machine-sewn methods for mass production
  • Built an electromagnet test module with an ESP32 microcontroller, MOSFET switching, and a rotary encoder to control the strength, length, and frequency of each pulse
