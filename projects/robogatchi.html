Robogatchi

2026 · Robotics · Mechatronics

Robogotchi, inspired by Tamagotchi, is a robotic pet that follows its users & requires real-time care. Built for ME235, Design of Microprocessor-Based Mechanical Systems, Robogotchi requires regular feeding and petting or else its food and mood health bar would decrease towards death. Mood is expressed through OLED eyes and audio feedback. Robogotchi uses color tracking to follow its user and with a custom made 3-D printed burger with an embedded RFID, you would be able to tap to feed him. Through a touch sensor, the Robogotchi can be petted to increase its mood and happiness. A GUI is paired with the Robogotchi, which displays the companions hatching, character customization, mood, and hunger and health.

  • Designed a 3D-printed differential-drive chassis for a pet robot, giving it a soft, character-like shell over a clear acrylic base with two driven wheels and a front caster
  • Integrated 8+ electromechanical subsystems around a single microcontroller, including drive motors, sensing, and animated OLED eyes, balancing them against real-time performance requirements
  • Solved packaging constraints for wheel clearance, a sliding access panel, and wiring routing, separating the body into an electronics bay and a battery bay for easy access
  • Soldered and assembled the robot's protoboard electronics, and tested interactive behaviors such as following a ball across the floor
  • Designed a browser-based control GUI served directly from the robot’s Wi-Fi access point, displaying live life bars, difficulty modes, a revive control, and a real-time target-tracking overlay, and opening with a three-click egg hatch animation to gamify the user experience
