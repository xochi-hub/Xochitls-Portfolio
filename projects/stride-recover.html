Stride Recover

2026 · Wearables · EMG Sensing · Biomechanics

Stride Recover is a smart compression sleeve that helps recreational athletes return to their sport after a hamstring injury. Most athletes rely on guesswork and feeling to decide when they are ready, and about 30% reinjure within two weeks of returning to play. Through interviews, we found that users felt caught between wanting to get back to their sport and fearing another injury. Built for Human-AI Design Methods with a team of four, the sleeve uses electromyography (EMG) sensing to track muscle activation and fatigue in real time. Since normal muscle activation varies from person to person, Stride Recover compares the injured leg to the healthy one, and sends a subtle haptic alert when the user is at risk of overexertion. Over time, the data builds a personal profile that guides a weekly rehab plan.

  • Designed a smart hamstring compression sleeve with EMG sensing to track muscle activation, fatigue, and bilateral symmetry in real time
  • Conducted user interviews with recreational athletes to understand recovery fears, shaping a low-friction design worn like a standard compression garment
  • Prototyped a companion display on a protoboard with an OLED screen to show real-time biofeedback and recovery data
  • Designed threshold-based haptic alerts that only activate when meaningful limits are reached, reducing alarm fatigue so users don’t start ignoring warnings
