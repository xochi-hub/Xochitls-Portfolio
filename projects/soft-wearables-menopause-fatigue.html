Soft Wearables for Menopause Fatigue

2026 · Soft Wearables · Mechanical Design

More than 70% of women will experience musculoskeletal symptoms during menopause; 25% will be disabled by them. Menopause symptoms cause underlying fatigue that affect daily life. The topic of menopause is commonly stigmitized, causing generations of women to be under-resourced and unprepared for the perimenopause transition. Being able to understand and collect data about your body with a wearable can help women plan alongside their fatigue, improving their quality of life.

  • Synthesized research across five fields (menopause endocrinology, musculoskeletal decline, menstrual cycle fatigue, soma design, and wearable sensing) to translate dynamic movement into a measurable signal of exhaustion
  • Designed and ran a multi-day user study using take-home kits with journals, markers, a phone stand, and a body mapping page where participants marked where exhaustion sits, what it feels like, and when it arrives
  • Bridged user research and hardware by translating body maps into sensor placement and selecting hardware built for all-day, low-power wear
  • Prototyped soft sensing and feedback samples using conductive fabric and thread, IMUs, a piezoelectric sensor with a buffer/filter stage, and inflatables for pneumatic pressure, exploring three directions: expressive surfaces, continuous sensing, and reactive touch.
