Formaflow

2025 · Morphing Wearables · Robotics

Formaflow is an auto-lifting, silhouette-altering dress with an integrated IMU. By detecting when the wearer climbs stairs, the dress lifts to provide clearance so the wearer can walk, climb stairs, and cross wet ground without tripping or dragging the fabric. Built for ME 292C, Design with Morphing Materials and Mechanisms, with a team of four, the dress has two layers that hide the mechanisms inside. Four servo motors pull threads that gather the hem upward, and two servo motors curl octopus-inspired TPU flippers outward to make more room for the feet. The wearer can also trigger the lift with a handheld remote.

  • Sewed the full double-layer dress and designed a structural corset to hold the electronics and motors, with hidden channels for thread routing and a hatch inside the lining for access
  • Tested thread and fabric samples before building the full dress, choosing cotton thread on a midweight cotton blend because elastic thread tangled and rigid cord jammed in the pulleys
  • Added taped-thread stoppers along the lifting lines to control where the fabric folds and stop it from over-lifting, making the hem lift the same way each time
  • Iterated 3D-printed TPU attachments sewn into the dress, moving from interlocking chevron pieces that collapsed on the moving fabric to a square attachment that created a consistent scrunch
  • Integrated six servo motors, an ESP32, and an IMU into the garment, lifting the hem about 120 mm to clear the ground while walking
